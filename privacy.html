01

Introduction

Welcome to Rolati: Cine Reels Scout ("we", "our", "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application Rolati: Cine Reels Scout ("the App") handles information when you use it.

02

Information We Do NOT Collect

Rolati: Cine Reels Scout is designed with your privacy in mind. We want to be transparent that:

  • We do not require user registration or login.
  • We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information (such as name, email address, phone number, or location).
  • We do not use any analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not display advertisements or use advertising SDKs.
  • We do not sell, share, or rent any user data to third parties.
03

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only using standard device storage (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us:

  • Language preference — Your selected content language.
  • Favorites list — Movies and TV shows you have saved to your favorites.
  • Game scores — Your scores from the Blurry Reveal guessing game.
  • Onboarding status — Whether you have completed the initial onboarding.
You can clear your favorites data at any time through the Settings screen within the App. Uninstalling the App will remove all locally stored data.
04

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services to provide its core functionality:

The Movie Database (TMDB) API

We use the TMDB API to retrieve movie and TV show information, including titles, descriptions, images, ratings, cast details, and trailer metadata. TMDB's privacy policy is available at: https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy

YouTube

Movie and TV show trailers are played through embedded YouTube videos. When you watch a trailer, YouTube's standard terms and privacy policy apply. YouTube's privacy policy is available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Note: We do not control the data practices of these third-party services. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.
05

Notifications

The App may request your permission to send local notifications to remind you about upcoming movie release dates. These notifications are generated entirely on your device. No notification data is sent to any external server. You can disable notifications at any time through your device's Settings.

06

Children's Privacy

Rolati: Cine Reels Scout does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect personal data, the App is safe for users of all ages.

07

Data Security

Since all user preferences and data are stored locally on your device and we do not collect or transmit any personal data, there is no risk of data breach from our servers.

08

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
